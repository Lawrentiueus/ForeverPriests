A high-contrast Priest assistant for tracking the 5-Second Rule, Spirit Tap timers, and wand execution thresholds. SpiritTap Engine is a lightweight, high-contrast WoW Classic/Forever addon built specifically for Priests to help maximize mana regen and level much more efficiently.

Best Professions for Priests

Professions received a major overhaul in Forever, bringing a wealth of new recipes and mechanics—including beneficial campsite contributions. While almost all professions offer advantages, four stand out as exceptional choices for Priests:

Tailoring

Since Priests exclusively wear Cloth gear, Tailoring is a natural fit. Forever introduces a heap of new patterns ranging from school-specific damage gear to level 10 Belts that impact Spell Power.

Enchanting

Ideal for self-sufficiency, Enchanters can upgrade their own gear and craft early-game wands, positioning you as an indispensable asset to your guild thanks to a slate of new enchanting formulas.

Engineering

A classic favorite that becomes even stronger in Forever due to new schematics, utility gadgets, and specialized engineering trinkets.

Alchemy

Priests have always relied heavily on consumables, and Forever makes this profession even more enticing with new recipes designed to buff stats, boost damage output, or provide crowd-control utility.

Profession Pairing Tip During leveling, pair your crafting choice with its matching gathering profession (such as Herbalism for Alchemy or Mining for Engineering). Once level-capped and established, you can drop the gathering profession for a second crafting path.

PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS

Spec Comparison: Strengths & Weaknesses

Side-by-side analysis of how each Priest spec performs across WoW Forever content.

Discipline Priest

S-Tier Utility & DPS Hybrid

Core Strengths

  • Power Word: Shield prevents lethal damage before it lands.
  • Power Infusion provides massive damage/healing burst windows for allies.
  • Unlocks a viable Smite DPS hybrid playstyle via Power in Light and Holy Precision.

Key Weaknesses

  • Lower raw AOE burst output compared to Holy in raid settings.
  • Requires careful mana management on extended boss encounters.

Holy Priest

S-Tier Raid Healing

Core Strengths

  • Unmatched raid healing throughput powered by Prayer of Mending and Prayer of Healing.
  • Strong mana recovery through Litany of Light.
  • Versatile toolkit capable of adapting to tank-healing or broad raid stabilization.

Key Weaknesses

  • High mana expenditure during intense encounter damage phases without downranking.
  • Vulnerable in PvP when focused without direct mobility cooldowns.

Shadow Priest

S-Tier Ranged Burst & Utility

Core Strengths

  • Revamped Shadowform increases Shadow critical strike chance while lowering spell mana costs.
  • High sustained single-target DPS powered by Improved Mind Flay and Shadow Word: Death.
  • Shadow Weaving delivers key shadow damage amplification for the raid.

Key Weaknesses

  • Lack of movement tools forces stationary "turret" positioning to maintain DPS output.
  • Vulnerable to mana starvation on long encounters if spell casting is unoptimized.
